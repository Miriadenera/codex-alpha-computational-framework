# AI Modules

This directory contains AI-assisted modules developed for exploratory candidate-level analysis inside the Codex Alpha Computational Framework.

The AI layer is designed to support:

- unsupervised anomaly detection;
- candidate-level anomaly ranking;
- multidimensional feature contribution analysis;
- AI-assisted interpretation of catalogue-derived indicators;
- prioritization of sources requiring external validation;
- future explainable scoring workflows;
- future uncertainty-aware candidate triage.

The objective of this layer is not to impose predefined astrophysical classifications and not to claim direct astrophysical discovery.

Its purpose is to assist the exploratory analysis of high-dimensional astronomical datasets, starting from Gaia DR3, by helping identify which sources may deserve deeper inspection through external validation services.

Current implementation includes:

- unsupervised anomaly detection through Isolation Forest.

Current outputs must be interpreted as candidate-level prioritization indicators.

They are not final classifications and do not confirm planets, binary systems, hidden companions, black holes, exotic objects or other astrophysical interpretations.

Future modules may progressively expand toward:

- explainable candidate scoring;
- graph-assisted candidate ranking;
- uncertainty-aware anomaly interpretation;
- larger Gaia-derived dataset analysis;
- AI-assisted validation planning;
- structured candidate dossier interpretation;
- human-in-the-loop scientific review.

All AI-assisted outputs require independent validation through external astronomical services such as Gaia Archive, SIMBAD, VizieR, Aladin, X-Match and related catalogue infrastructures.
